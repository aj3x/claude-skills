# References

Sources consulted or relied on. Principles are paraphrased in SKILL.md.

## Primary
- Dieter Rams, "Ten principles for good design" (Vitsoe: https://www.vitsoe.com/gb/about/good-design). Innovative, useful, aesthetic, understandable, unobtrusive, honest, long-lasting, thorough, environmentally friendly, as little design as possible.
- Laws of UX, Jon Yablonski (https://lawsofux.com): Aesthetic-Usability Effect, Choice Overload, Chunking, Cognitive Load, Doherty Threshold, Fitts's Law, Goal-Gradient, Hick's Law, Jakob's Law, Gestalt laws (Common Region, Proximity, Prägnanz, Similarity, Uniform Connectedness), Miller's Law, Peak-End Rule, Postel's Law, Serial Position, Tesler's Law, Von Restorff, Zeigarnik.
- WCAG 2.2 (https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): contrast, keyboard, target size, reduced motion guidance.

## The shared mindset
The common thread: design is problem-solving for a person's task; reduce to the essential; honesty of material and purpose; systems over one-offs; restraint so the object serves rather than performs.

- Louis Sullivan, "The Tall Office Building Artistically Considered" (1896): form ever follows function. Bauhaus carried it into teaching.
- Braun / Ulm: Hans Gugelot, Fritz Eichler, Dietrich Lubs (Braun design colleagues of Rams); Otl Aicher and Max Bill (Ulm School of Design, systematic, rational design).
- Massimo Vignelli, "The Vignelli Canon": discipline, a small set of typefaces, grids, semantic/syntactic/pragmatic rigour.
- Josef Müller-Brockmann, "Grid Systems in Graphic Design": objectivity, grids, clarity.
- Naoto Fukasawa and Jasper Morrison, "Super Normal" (2006): objects that feel inevitable and ordinary.
- Kenya Hara and Muji, "Designing Design" and "White": emptiness as receptive space.
- Bruno Munari, "Design as Art" and "Das Gute Design": solve with the least means, no styling.
- Edward Tufte, "The Visual Display of Quantitative Information": data-ink ratio, chartjunk, small multiples.
- Don Norman, "The Design of Everyday Things": affordances, signifiers, feedback, mapping, conceptual models.
- Jony Ive: public talks on care, simplicity as resolved complexity, material honesty (Ive acknowledged Rams' influence).
- Steve Krug, "Don't Make Me Think".
- Luke Wroblewski, "Web Form Design" and "Mobile First".
- Brad Frost, "Atomic Design".
- Bruce Tognazzini, "First Principles of Interaction Design" (asktog.com).
